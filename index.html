What is calculated

📈

Calibration

Analyte/IS area ratio vs concentration, 1/x² weighted. Standards outside ±15 % (±20 % at LLOQ) are dropped one at a time; LOD/LOQ, ion ratios and the working range are reported.

🧪

Quantification

Several calibrations per run — each analyte takes the curve it was actually spiked in. Dilution and IS amount are explicit. Relative mode (% of t₀) when no IS matches.

⏱

Kinetics & statistics

Mean ± SD/SEM, % remaining per replicate, Welch's t-test for 0 vs 24 h, first-order k with 95 % CI and t½, rate vs chain length.
