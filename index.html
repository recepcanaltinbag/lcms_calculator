What it does

Calculation

  • Reads Analyst/SCIEX exports: Sample Name plus Compound-1 (quantifier) and -2 (qualifier) areas. Misspelt compound names and rows glued by a missing line break are repaired and reported.
  • Internal-standard ratio calibration, 1/x² weighted, with ±15 % / ±20 % (LLOQ) acceptance and automatic exclusion of failing standards.
  • Several calibrations per experiment: each analyte takes the first curve where it actually responds.
  • Sample names such as sap_c12_t24_r1, C8B_d1_1 or T14_20m_2 are parsed into condition, substrate, time and replicate.
  • Relative mode (% of t₀) when no matching internal standard was spiked.

Quality control

  • Internal standard drift across the run, and the IS response of samples versus standards.
  • Flags for missing IS, below LLOQ, above ULOQ, at blank level and off-ratio qualifier ions.
  • Solvent blanks tabulated to expose carry-over.
  • Re-injections handled; single injections can be excluded with a click and exported to the configuration.
